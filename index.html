Research question

How do we make space systems more sustainable when technical capability, economic incentives, and governance evolve at different speeds?

My research sits between engineering and institutions: modeling orbital environments, examining system-level risk, and asking how emerging technologies can be translated into practical governance.

About

I came to space through policy—and stayed because the hardest questions require both technical and institutional thinking.

My academic path began in political science, history, and sociology before moving into graduate study in space operations and systems. That combination shapes the questions I ask: not only whether a technology works, but how it changes incentives, access, risk, governance, and the long-term sustainability of the space environment.

I am particularly interested in PhD work that bridges space systems, computational modeling, technology policy, AI governance, and complex sociotechnical systems.
